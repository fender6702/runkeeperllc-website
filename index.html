Wei Zhang

Founder & CEO

Wei Zhang has more than 20 years of experience in internet products, FinTech and digital retail. He was a founding team member of Alibaba's Hema (Freshhippo), where he led product and operations, and later served as General Manager at a Ping An Group technology subsidiary.

Earlier he managed products at Tencent, PPS.tv, Renren and Baidu, and founded technology companies in China and the Philippines. He holds a degree from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications.
